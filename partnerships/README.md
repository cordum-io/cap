# CAP Design-Partner Kit (DRAFT)

DRAFT — DO NOT SEND OR PUBLISH WITHOUT EXPLICIT HUMAN APPROVAL

This directory holds **internal, draft-only** materials for a possible CAP design-partner
program. Nothing here has been approved, and nothing here may be sent, published, or
acted on without explicit, per-instance human approval recorded via the
[approval checklist](approval-checklist.md).

## Hard guardrails (apply to every asset here)

These are non-negotiable and are enforced in spirit by the claim linter and in practice by
the approval checklist:

- **No outbound action without human approval.** No sending, publishing, bulk or
  autonomous outreach, tracking pixels, CRM/calendar automation, or paid data enrichment.
- **No named targets.** The [target-account rubric](target-account-rubric.md) is
  *criteria only*. No lists of real companies or people live in this repository.
- **No promises we cannot keep.** No exclusivity, no guaranteed governance/maintainer
  seat, no certification, endorsement, or legal/foundation commitment.
- **No fabricated metrics, adopters, quotes, or logos.** A partner's name, quote, or logo
  is used only with that partner's recorded, explicit consent (see
  [case-study template](case-study-template.md) and
  [ADOPTERS.md](../ADOPTERS.md)).
- **What partners actually get:** bounded engineering and TCK help, and the ordinary
  ability to file issues and RFCs like any contributor — nothing more.
- **Governance separation.** A design partner is not a maintainer and gains no governance
  authority. Maintainership follows [MAINTAINERS.md](../MAINTAINERS.md) only.

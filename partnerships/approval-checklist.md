# CAP Outreach Approval Checklist (DRAFT)

DRAFT — DO NOT SEND OR PUBLISH WITHOUT EXPLICIT HUMAN APPROVAL

**No outreach or publication happens until a human completes this checklist for the
specific recipient, channel, and message.** An agent must never send, schedule, or bulk-
process outreach. This checklist is the gate; a blank checklist means "do not send."

## Per-instance approval (one per recipient + channel + message)

- **Recipient (one human):** _____
- **Channel:** ☐ email ☐ forum reply ☐ other: _____
- **Exact message** (link to the approved draft, verbatim): _____
- **Approver (human) + date:** _____

### The message must be confirmed to have NONE of:

- ☐ named third-party adopters/partners, or any quote/logo without recorded consent
- ☐ certification, endorsement, exclusivity, guaranteed governance/maintainer seat
- ☐ legal, foundation, roadmap, or support-tier promises
- ☐ fabricated or unverifiable metrics, urgency, or scarcity
- ☐ personal/sensitive data, or data from paid enrichment
- ☐ tracking pixel, analytics link, CRM/calendar automation

### And must be confirmed to BE:

- ☐ addressed to a single human who has a plausible, public technical reason to hear from us
- ☐ honest about what the program is and is not (per the [offer](design-partner-offer.md))
- ☐ sent manually by a human — no bulk, no automation, no autonomous agent send

## Logging

- **Sent by (human):** _____  **When:** _____
- One approval covers one message to one recipient. Reuse requires a new checklist.

## Standing prohibitions

Regardless of approval: no adding target accounts/logos/quotes to the repo, no announcing
adopters/partners, no foundation submission, no reserving a governance seat, and no
outbound action taken autonomously by an agent.

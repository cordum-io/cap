# CAP Case-Study Template (DRAFT)

DRAFT — DO NOT SEND OR PUBLISH WITHOUT EXPLICIT HUMAN APPROVAL

A template for a partner-authored or partner-approved case study. **A case study may be
published only with the partner's explicit, recorded consent** (see the consent block).
Do not draft claims on a partner's behalf and present them as theirs.

## Consent block (required before any publication)

- **Organization:** _____
- **Approving person + role:** _____
- **Consent recorded:** ☐ yes — link/date: _____
- **Approved to use:** ☐ name ☐ logo ☐ quote ☐ metrics (each opt-in, none assumed)
- **Scope of approval:** _____ (channels/pages where it may appear)
- **Withdrawal:** the partner may withdraw consent at any time; on withdrawal the asset is
  unpublished.

## Content (partner-verified facts only)

- **Problem:** what they were solving.
- **Why CAP:** the specific capability that mattered.
- **What they built:** profile/version used, real (non-demo) workload.
- **Result:** partner-verified outcomes. **No fabricated or rounded-up metrics.**
- **Quote:** verbatim, partner-approved, attributed with consent.

## Do-not

- Do not imply certification, endorsement, exclusivity, or a governance relationship.
- Do not use the study as adopter evidence for [readiness](../governance/READINESS.md)
  unless it independently meets the adopter evidence rules (public, consented, non-demo,
  partner-controlled).

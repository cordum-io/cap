# CAP Design-Partner Offer (DRAFT)

DRAFT — DO NOT SEND OR PUBLISH WITHOUT EXPLICIT HUMAN APPROVAL

A draft description of a possible design-partner engagement. Not an agreement, not an
offer of terms, and not to be shared without approval.

## What a design partner gets

- **Bounded engineering help** integrating a real workload with CAP — time-boxed to the
  [integration sprint](integration-sprint-guide.md).
- **TCK help**: assistance running the conformance TCK against the partner's
  implementation and interpreting the self-test report.
- **Direct feedback channel**: the ordinary ability to file issues and RFCs, with a
  maintainer shepherd for their proposals (same [RFC process](../rfcs/README.md) as any
  contributor — no special decision rights).

## What a design partner does NOT get

- **No exclusivity** of any kind.
- **No governance or maintainer seat.** Design partners have no vote and no authority;
  maintainership follows [MAINTAINERS.md](../MAINTAINERS.md).
- **No certification, endorsement, or "official" status.** A conformance self-report is
  self-hosted TCK evidence only — never certification.
- **No legal, foundation, roadmap, or support-tier commitments.**
- **No use of their name, logo, or quote** unless they give explicit, recorded consent
  per the [case-study template](case-study-template.md).

## What we ask in return (optional, never required)

- Honest feedback and bug reports.
- *If they choose*, a consent-based public statement of use that could count toward
  [readiness](../governance/READINESS.md) adopter evidence. This is entirely optional and
  partner-controlled; we never author an adopter statement on their behalf.

## Boundaries

Participation is subject to the [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and involves no
exchange of personal or sensitive data beyond what the integration technically requires.
Any real engagement requires human approval and, where relevant, counsel review.

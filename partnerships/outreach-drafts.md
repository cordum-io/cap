# CAP Outreach Drafts (DRAFT)

DRAFT — DO NOT SEND OR PUBLISH WITHOUT EXPLICIT HUMAN APPROVAL

Unsent message drafts. These are **templates with placeholders**, not addressed to anyone,
and must not be sent, scheduled, or bulk-processed. Every send requires a completed
[approval checklist](approval-checklist.md). No tracking pixels, link shorteners with
analytics, or CRM automation.

## Guardrails baked into every draft

- Honest and specific; no fabricated metrics, adopters, urgency, or scarcity.
- No promise of exclusivity, certification, endorsement, a governance seat, or legal/
  foundation outcomes.
- One human recipient at a time; no bulk send, no autonomous send, no list import.

get sent.

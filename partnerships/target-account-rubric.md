# CAP Design-Partner Fit Rubric (DRAFT)

DRAFT — DO NOT SEND OR PUBLISH WITHOUT EXPLICIT HUMAN APPROVAL

Criteria for evaluating whether an organization is a good **technical** fit for a design
partnership. This is a **rubric only**. It contains **no named companies or people**, and
this repository must never hold a target list, contact data, or any enriched/purchased
personal data.

## Fit criteria (technical, not commercial)

- **Real multi-agent or distributed-execution need** — they already run, or are building,
  systems where a job/scheduler/worker/safety split is a genuine fit.
- **Existing framework use** — e.g. a graph/agent framework where a CAP adapter would
  remove real glue code.
- **Willing to run a real workload** — not just a demo; able to commit to a bounded sprint.
- **Operates its own infrastructure** — can stand up NATS + an SDK in its environment.
- **Open-source-friendly** — comfortable filing issues/RFCs in the open.

## Explicit exclusions

- Do **not** score or prioritize by deal size, logo value, or "influence."
- Do **not** record named prospects, contacts, or any personal/sensitive data here.
- Fit is a **necessary** filter for *who to consider asking for approval to contact* — it
  is never permission to contact anyone. Contact happens only through the
  [approval checklist](approval-checklist.md).

## How to use

Evaluate a *category* of user (by workload shape, not by name) against the criteria.
Bring the category — never a named list — to the human approver, who decides whether any
outreach is authorized and to whom.
